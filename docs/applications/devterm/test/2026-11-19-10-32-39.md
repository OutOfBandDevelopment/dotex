## Findings

- All four bench hosts answer ping; the test failures are empty replies, not connection errors.
- The 2230 bridge answers a hand-sent query, so the `BenchTcpInstrument` post-connect delay (175 ms) is the suspect; the HP 34401A may need to be switched to remote or its serial bridge reset.
- Korad ports COM6 and COM7 are not enumerated by Windows now.
